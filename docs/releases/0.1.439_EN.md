# Sable Life 0.1.439

[中文更新说明](0.1.439.md)

- **Propulsion** adds a direction reference. Marked face directions remain the default and rotate with the part. World directions offers North, South, East, West, Up and Down, independent of body rotation. Horizontal and vertical angles offset the selected direction; force still acts at the marked point.
- Regular and Inspection Tool previews use the same direction as thrust. World-direction previews retain their orientation as the part turns.
- The **Inspection Tool** uses the authored 16×16 texture and ordinary handheld item model.
- Existing Propulsion modules retain their marked-face direction, angles and force settings. World direction settings persist through saves, copies and blueprints. Both manuals are updated.
